## Age and content
Sentence is intended for players aged **13 and over**. It deals with prison life: violence is implied, never shown in detail; there is no drug or tobacco use. Your age is asked at first launch and only the age bracket is kept on your device.

## Data and services used by Sentence
- **Local save:** your progress is stored on your device, in an encrypted file. As a guest, it never leaves your device.
- **Google Play Games (optional):** sign in to unlock achievements and appear on the leaderboards (one per prison). Your progress is also backed up with Google Play Games saved games (cloud save) on your Google account, so you can get it back on another device or after reinstalling; if the two copies differ, the game asks which one to keep. Purchases are not part of this copy: they are restored from Google Play.
- **Google Play Billing:** for in-app purchases. Pixel & Quill Studio never receives your payment details. Each purchase is checked with Google Play by a verification service on Cloudflare (product ID and purchase token only).
- **Google AdMob and UMP:** banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads, with a consent form in Europe. Ads are limited to content rated “T” (Teen) or lower. Players under 15 only get non-personalised ads. Manage your choices anytime in **Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage ad choices**.
- **Permissions:** Sentence requests no sensitive permissions (no location, contacts, camera or microphone).

## Purchases in Sentence
Sentence is free to play at Valmont Prison. Other prisons, extra inmates, jokers (virtual items with no monetary value, used to undo a choice), an ad-free option, two bundles (inmates + no ads, all prisons) and a Premium Pack are available as optional in-app purchases. Prices are shown in your currency on Google Play and in the in-game shop. A few jokers can also be earned by watching an optional rewarded ad.

Non-consumable purchases can be restored with “Restore purchases”.
