## Development status
One Last Heist is in production at Pixel & Quill Studio. Its release date and store listing will be published on this page when the game comes out. The information below describes the version planned for release.

## Age and content
One Last Heist is fiction about heists: crimes, the police, arrests and implied violence, in a light tone. It is not intended for children under 13: at first launch, the game asks for your year of birth only, keeps just an age bracket on your device, and stays locked under 13. Between 13 and 17, it reminds you to ask a parent or legal guardian before playing and before any purchase.

## Data and services
- **Guest or Google Play Games:** play as a guest and your progress stays on your device. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and adds achievements, the global leaderboard and, later, cloud save.
- **Ads (Google AdMob):** banners, an ad between two jobs (never during a job or the tutorial) and optional rewarded ads. In Europe, Google's consent form records your choice; you can change it in **My account → Manage ad choices**. Under 15, only non-personalised ads.
- **Purchases (Google Play Billing)** and **anti-cheat (Google Play Integrity)**. The studio never sees your payment details.
- **Planned, not active yet:** cloud save and game servers, analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase). The privacy policy will be updated before any of them is switched on.

## Purchases
Most of the game is free. Optional purchases: redo tokens, four extra targets, four exclusive specialists, the family lawyer, ad removal, or a premium bundle. Prices will be shown in the game and on Google Play.

## Legal documents
Read the [privacy policy](/one-last-heist/privacy/) and the [terms of use](/one-last-heist/terms/) of One Last Heist. In the game, you'll find them in **My account → Legal documents**, and **My account → Delete my account and data** erases everything at any time.
