Sentence

Prison survival

Coming November 2026

Swipe card game: pick your prison, your inmate, and survive as many days as you can.

Every day, one card, two choices: swipe left or right. Keep your gauges balanced, because an empty gauge is deadly, and so is one that is too full. Hold on until the end of your sentence.

You pick your prison, then your character, man or woman, then you hear your verdict. Every inmate has their own story, exclusive cards and endings.

Your sentence starts at Valmont Prison, free to play: the yard, the workshops, the crews. Then come the paid prisons, each with its own rules, its exclusive inmate and its cards: the high-security prison at Mornac, the island prison at Karreg, the semi-liberty centre at Saint-Aubin, a prison abroad, in Sarvania, an imaginary country, and the old fortress of Vauclair, a 17th-century citadel that closes on 30 June.

“You’ve just been locked up. Survive as many days as you can.”

— a line from the game

In the game

Good to know

Age and content

Sentence is intended for players aged 13 and over. It deals with prison life: violence is implied, never shown in detail; there is no drug or tobacco use. Your age is asked at first launch and only the age bracket is kept on your device.

Data and services used by Sentence

  • Local save: your progress is stored only on your device, in an encrypted file.
  • Google Play Games (optional): sign in to unlock achievements, appear on the leaderboards (one per prison) and sync your save. Per-character records stay on your device.
  • Google Play Billing: for in-app purchases. Pixel & Quill Studio never receives your payment details.
  • Google AdMob and UMP: banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads, with a consent form in Europe. Ads are limited to content rated “T” (Teen) or lower. Players under 15 only get non-personalised ads. Manage your choices anytime in Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage ad choices.
  • Permissions: Sentence requests no sensitive permissions (no location, contacts, camera or microphone).

Purchases in Sentence

Sentence is free to play at Valmont Prison. Optional purchases, at the prices in euros planned at release (Google Play shows the final price in your currency):

  • Paid prisons: €2.99 each, with their exclusive inmate: high security, the island prison, the semi-liberty centre, the prison abroad and the old fortress.
  • Extra inmates at Valmont: €0.99 each (the ex-cop, the boxer, the white-collar swindler, the innocent, the old robber).
  • Jokers (virtual items with no monetary value, used to undo a choice): 5 for €0.99, 20 for €2.99. A few jokers can also be earned by watching an optional rewarded ad.
  • Remove ads: €3.99, for life. Rewarded ads stay available if you want them.
  • Premium Pack: €17.99: The whole game: every prison, no ads for life, all 5 paid inmates, 20 jokers.

Non-consumable purchases can be restored with “Restore purchases”.
