1. Who we are

Sentence is an Android mobile game published by Styvens Wentz, an individual entrepreneur (EI, Entrepreneur individuel) trading as Pixel & Quill Studio (“we”). Styvens Wentz EI is the data controller for the processing described below, except where Google acts as an independent controller for its own services (Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, AdMob).

2. What we do not do

  • Sentence does not ask you to create an account: we have no account system of our own and no server that receives your data.
  • We do not directly collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or photos.
  • We do not sell your data.

3. Data processed

3.1 Local save (on your device)

Your progress (days survived, scores, unlocked achievements, purchased inmates and prisons, jokers), your settings (language, sound, vibration) and the inmate name you choose are stored only on your device, in an encrypted file. We have no access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3.2 Google Play Games (optional)

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional: you can play in guest mode without signing in (without the global leaderboards or synced achievements). If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer name, achievements, the scores submitted to the leaderboards (one per prison) and, where applicable, a copy of your save (“saved games”) to sync it across your devices. This data is processed by Google under its own rules: Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (inmates, prisons, jokers, Premium Pack, ad removal) are made through Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives from Google a confirmation of the purchase (product ID, purchase token) in order to unlock the content.

3.4 Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (UMP)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads to earn jokers). AdMob may process your device’s advertising ID, your IP address, technical information (model, OS, language) and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform – UMP, compliant with the IAB TCF framework) is shown at first launch: - if you accept, personalised ads may be shown; - if you decline, only non-personalised ads (based on context) are shown, with processing limited to permitted purposes (serving, measurement, fraud prevention).

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage ad choices. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Buying ad removal (or the Premium Pack) turns off banners and interstitials; rewarded ads remain optional. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Recipients and transfers

The only recipients are Google services (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): Play Games, Play Billing, AdMob and UMP, as well as AdMob’s advertising partners to the extent of your consent. Some data may be transferred outside the European Union (notably to the United States); Google safeguards these transfers with the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. Retention

  • Local save: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Play Games, Play Billing and AdMob data: according to Google’s retention periods.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as needed to handle your request, then 3 years at most.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, the right to withdraw your consent at any time, and the right to give instructions on what happens to your data after your death. As we hold no data about you on our servers, most of these rights are exercised directly: - by clearing the game’s data or uninstalling it (local save); - via Manage ad choices (ads); - via your Google account (Play Games, purchases, advertising ID).

For any question, email us at pixelandquillstudio.pro@gmail.com; we reply within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr) or with the data protection authority of your country.

8. Children

Sentence deals with prison life (implied violence, offences and crime) and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security

The local save is encrypted. Communications with Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). The game requests no sensitive permissions (location, contacts, camera, microphone).

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when a new feature is added. The date at the top of this page will be updated and, for significant changes, you will be informed in the game.
